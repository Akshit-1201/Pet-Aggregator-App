Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: cancel a service before the day it's booked for, or a homestay at least 24 hours before check-in, and the full price of the service or stay is refunded to your original payment method. The Pawgo service fee is not refunded — unless the problem was on our side.

Services (walkers, sitters, groomers, trainers, behaviourists)

  • Before the day of the booking: cancel in the app (My bookings → Cancel) for a full refund of the service rate.
  • On or after the day of the booking: no refund.
  • The Pawgo service fee (10% of the rate) is not refunded.

Example: a ₹100 walk booked for Saturday costs ₹110. Cancelled on Friday, ₹100 is refunded. Cancelled on Saturday, nothing is refunded.

Homestays

  • Before you pay: you can cancel a request, or an accepted stay you haven't paid for yet, at no cost.
  • After you've paid, at least 24 hours before check-in: cancel in the app for a full refund of the stay (nightly price × nights).
  • Less than 24 hours before check-in, or after check-in: no refund.
  • The Pawgo service fee (₹150 per booking) is not refunded.

Example: a 3-night stay at ₹900 a night costs ₹2,850 (₹2,700 + ₹150 fee). Cancelled 2 days before check-in, ₹2,700 is refunded.

When the problem is on our side

If a booking fails because of us — for example, the time slot you were paying for was taken before your payment completed — the whole amount, including the service fee, is refunded automatically.

If a pro or host cancels or doesn't turn up, contact us at [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] and we will refund you in full.

How long refunds take

Refunds are issued immediately when you cancel, back to the payment method you used, through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5–7 working days to show them on your statement; UPI refunds are often faster. You'll see the refunded amount on the booking in the app.

Home Brands orders

Orders from Home Brands are arranged and paid directly between you and the brand in chat, not through Pawgo, so this policy doesn't cover them. Agree returns and refunds with the brand before you order. If a brand doesn't deliver or misleads you, report it from its page and we'll look into it.

For pros and hosts

When a booking is cancelled with a refund, the matching payout to you is cancelled too. Payouts are held until the day after a booking is completed so that refunds never have to be clawed back from you.
