Delete your account

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Pawgo Pets and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm it's you — with your password, your Google or Apple account, or a code sent to your phone, depending on how you sign in.

Your account is deleted straight away. This can't be undone.

Without the app

Email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] with the subject “Delete my Pawgo Pets account”, from the email address on your account, or include the phone number you sign in with. We will confirm the request is yours and delete the account within 30 days.

What happens to your data

DataWhat happens
Sign-in account, profile, pets, listings, swipes, notifications, device tokens, block listDeleted
Profile, pet and listing photos, and ID documentsDeleted
Posts, comments and reviewsKept, but shown as “Deleted user”, so conversations other people are reading still make sense
Bookings and payment recordsKept — they are financial records shared with the other party and needed for refunds and accounting
Payout account reference (pros and hosts)Kept so that earnings still owed to you can be paid
