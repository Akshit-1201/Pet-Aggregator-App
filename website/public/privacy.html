Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: we collect what the app needs to run — your account, your pets, your bookings and what you post — and nothing for advertising. We never sell your data. Card and UPI details go only to our payment processor, Razorpay. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Pawgo Pets (“Pawgo”, “we”) is an app for pet parents, pet care professionals and homestay hosts in Mumbai. It is operated by [[LEGAL_ENTITY]], [[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]]. This policy explains how we handle personal data in the Pawgo Pets app and on this website, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

We do not collect your device's location — the area is chosen from a list — and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

Who can see your data

  • Other Pawgo users see what is meant to be public: your name, area, pets, photos, listings, posts, comments and reviews. The person you chat with sees your messages, but phone numbers, email addresses and links in chat are hidden automatically.
  • The pro or host you book sees the booking details they need to look after your pet.
  • Pawgo staff see what is needed to run the service — for example reports, and ID documents during verification (through short-lived secure links).

Service providers we share it with

We share data only with providers that help us run Pawgo, and only what each one needs:

  • Razorpay — payments, refunds and partner payouts. Your card or UPI details are entered on Razorpay's checkout and never reach us.
  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) — sign-in, the app's database, file storage, notifications and server code. Uploaded photos are scanned by Google Cloud Vision to detect unsafe images.
  • Resend — sending emails such as booking receipts.
  • Google and Apple, if you choose to sign in with them.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We may disclose data when required by law or to protect the safety of users and their pets.

Where it is stored

The app's data and photos are stored on Google Cloud in the Mumbai region (asia-south1). Some service providers above may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Notifications are removed after 90 days.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, pets, listings, photos, ID documents, notifications and device tokens are deleted immediately. Posts, comments and reviews stay but are anonymised to “Deleted user”. Bookings and payment records are kept because they are financial records shared with the other party.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: view and edit your profile and pets in the app, or ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete account in the app. See how to delete your account, including without the app.
  • Notifications: switch categories on or off in Settings. Payment and account notices are always sent.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can stop using Pawgo and delete your account at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]]. We reply within 30 days.

Children

Pawgo Pets is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by role, payments are handled by a PCI-DSS compliant processor, and sensitive actions such as payments and refunds are carried out only on our servers. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
