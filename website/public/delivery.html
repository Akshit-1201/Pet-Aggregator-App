Service delivery policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: Pawgo Pets sells pet care services, not physical products, and ships nothing itself. Services are delivered in person, at the date and time you booked. Products from Home Brands are delivered by the brand, as agreed with you in chat.

Services

Walks, sitting visits, grooming and training sessions and behaviour consults are delivered in person by the pro you booked, at the date, time and duration shown on your booking. Your booking is confirmed in the app as soon as your payment succeeds, and you can message the pro there to arrange details such as the meeting point.

Homestays

Homestays take place at the host's home in Mumbai for the dates you booked. The stay is confirmed in the app once the host has accepted your request and you have paid. Drop-off and pick-up times are agreed with the host in chat.

Home Brands

Home Brands are independent businesses. Each brand's page says whether it delivers across Mumbai, to nearby areas only, or offers pickup. Delivery time, charges and address are agreed with the brand in chat, and the brand ships the order — Pawgo does not.

Confirmation and receipts

Every paid booking appears in the app under My bookings, and its receipt under Payments. You also get a notification when a booking is confirmed, and a reminder the day before.
