Booking care, start to finish

Pet parents book and pay inside the app. Pros and hosts set their own prices and hours.

Services by the slot

Each pro publishes weekly hours. You pick a free slot up to two weeks ahead, pay, and it's confirmed — two people can never book the same time.

Home Brands by chat

Small Mumbai businesses list their treats, food and accessories. You order by messaging the brand and pay them directly — Pawgo takes no fee and isn't part of the sale.

Homestays by request

Send a host your dates and your pet's details. You pay only after the host accepts, and the stay is confirmed once paid.

Safety built in

Phone numbers and links are hidden in chat, photos are checked for unsafe content, and anyone can be reported or blocked. Pros and hosts can earn a verified badge after an ID check.

Pricing

Pawgo Pets is free to download and use. When you book, you pay the pro's or host's price plus a Pawgo service fee, shown before you pay. All prices are in Indian rupees (INR).
